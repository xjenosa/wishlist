# Privacy

wwishe is built to collect as little as possible.

- **Your lists are yours, and we never see them.** wwishe has no server and no
  account, and nothing it keeps is ever sent to us. It keeps your lists in
  Chrome's own storage for extensions. We do not see them, we do not sell them,
  and we never use them to train anything, because none of it ever reaches us.
- **No analytics, no tracking**, and no account of wwishe's own. wwishe itself
  sends nothing over the internet.
- **Chrome sync, if your Chrome uses it.** When you are signed in to Chrome and
  it syncs your extensions, Chrome carries your lists, their order, the list you
  last opened and the last currency mark you typed to your other computers
  signed in to the same Google account, through Google's servers. That is
  Chrome's own sync, run by Google and not by us, and what it carries is subject
  to Google's privacy policy as well as this one. With it off, your lists stay
  on the one computer.
- **What stays on this computer only.** A list you delete goes to Recently
  deleted, which is kept on this computer alone, outside Chrome sync, and it
  goes for good 30 days after you deleted it. A change Chrome has not synced
  yet is kept on this computer too, until it has. Clearing Chrome's browsing
  data removes neither.
- **What wwishe can reach in Chrome.** It asks Chrome for three permissions, the
  side panel, storage and the clipboard. Only the clipboard shows a warning when
  you install it, “Read data you copy and paste”, and wwishe reads your
  clipboard only when you choose Paste in its right-click menu. It cannot read
  the pages you visit, your tabs, your history or your downloads.
- **Copy, Export and Import go only where you send them.** Copy puts a list on
  your clipboard when you press it. Export saves a text file where Chrome saves
  your downloads. Import reads only the file you pick.
- **Links that leave wwishe.** Contact starts an email to us in your own mail
  program, and nothing goes until you send it.
- **Deleting.** Removing wwishe from Chrome deletes everything it kept on that
  computer. When Chrome syncs your extensions, it also removes wwishe and your
  lists from every computer signed in to the same account, so export a list
  first if you want to keep it. Nothing of yours is anywhere else to delete,
  because nothing ever reached us.
- **When you write to us.** We use your address to answer you and for nothing
  else.
